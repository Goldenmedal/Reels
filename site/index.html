The methods

01
  1. 01

    Active recall

    Close the book. Write down everything you remember, then check what you missed.

  2. 02

    Spaced repetition

    Review right before you'd forget: 1 day, 3 days, a week. Anki does the scheduling.

  3. 03

    Interleaving

    Mix problem types instead of doing twenty of the same one in a row.

  4. 04

    Pretest yourself

    Guess the answers before you learn the topic. Getting it wrong first helps it stick.

  5. 05

    The Feynman technique

    Explain it like you're talking to a 12-year-old. Where you get stuck is what you don't know.

  6. 06

    Sleep on it

    Your brain saves what you learned while you sleep. All-nighters cost you more than they buy.

The rules

03

Rereading feels like studying. It isn't.
